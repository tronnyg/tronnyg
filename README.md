## 👋 About me

Full-Stack developer from Cape Town. I like exploring different things and topics, and I'm always working on something.

- 🧪 **Experimenter:** I like trying things just to see what happens, even when they break
- 🔨 **Builder:** when I'm curious about something, I make a small version of it, and that's how most of my projects start
- 📚 **Learner:** I'm happy being a beginner again, since every new area starts with not knowing much
- 🧭 **Explorer:** I move between different areas instead of staying in one lane
- 🌿 **Off the keyboard:** hiking, gaming, and watching films and listening to music
